About Om Sai Hospital

Welcome to Om Sai Hospital, where expert surgical skill meets compassionate patient care. Led by a highly experienced General Surgeon and Gynecologist, our hospital is committed to providing advanced, evidence-based treatments in a comfortable and safe environment.

From routine consultations to complex laparoscopic surgeries, we are here for every step of your health journey. Our patient-first approach ensures transparent communication, honest pricing, and personalised follow-up care.
